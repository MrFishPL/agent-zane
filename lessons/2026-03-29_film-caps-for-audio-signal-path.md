title: Use film capacitors in audio signal paths
date: 2026-03-29

## Context
User was designing a 4-channel audio mixer. Agent recommended Panasonic ECQ-E2104KF film caps for coupling/signal-path positions and noted ceramic alternatives for power bypass only.

## Feedback
User confirmed film caps for audio signal path is a general rule worth remembering for all audio projects.

## Generalized Rule
For any audio circuit, use film capacitors (polyester, polypropylene) in the signal path — coupling caps, feedback networks, filter caps. MLCC ceramic capacitors cause microphonic effects (mechanical vibration converts to electrical noise) in audio signal paths. Ceramic is acceptable only for power supply decoupling/bypass positions in audio circuits.
