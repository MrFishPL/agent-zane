---
title: Consolidate to single distributor when multi-source savings are marginal
date: 2026-03-29
type: preference
---

## Context
Agent recommended consolidating the entire audio mixer BOM at Mouser instead of splitting across Mouser + LCSC + DigiKey. The LCSC savings on passives was ~$30 on a $2000 order — not worth the extra shipping, invoicing, and lead time complexity.

## Feedback
User approved this approach. Save as a general sourcing strategy rule.

## Generalized Rule
When comparing multi-distributor vs. single-distributor sourcing, factor in shipping costs, invoice complexity, and lead time alignment — not just unit price. If the total savings from splitting across distributors is less than ~5% of total BOM cost, recommend consolidating at one distributor for simplicity. Always state the savings amount so the user can make an informed decision.
