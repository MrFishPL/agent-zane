## Generalized Rule
When sourcing panel-mount potentiometers for audio applications (volume controls, tone controls, faders), default to Alpha (Taiwan Alpha) brand. They offer the best combination of price, availability, and quality for audio panel-mount pots. Available at Mouser, DigiKey, and specialty audio suppliers like Tayda Electronics. Common series: RD901F (16mm), RV16 (16mm), RK09 (9mm).
